3. Treeline is free. We don't charge for it, show ads, or sell your data. If we ever start charging, we'll tell you first, and you won't owe anything unless you agree to pay.

## Who can use Treeline

1. You must be at least 18 and the parent or legal guardian of the kids in your treehouse. Any other grown-up you add, such as a sitter, must also be at least 18, and you're responsible for what they do in your treehouse.
2. Parent accounts are for people, not bots or scripts. You sign in with your own Google account. Don't share it, and don't sign in as someone else.
3. Kids don't get accounts. They use phones that a parent has signed in to the treehouse. You're responsible for which devices are signed in, and you can sign a device out at any time.

## Your responsibilities as a parent

1. **You decide who your kids can call.** A string between two treehouses only connects once parents in **both** treehouses approve it. Only approve strings to families you know and trust.
2. **You supervise your kids' use of Treeline.** We give you tools such as strings, quiet hours, and call history, but they don't replace your supervision. Treeline doesn't listen to or check what people say on calls.
3. **You keep your treehouse secure.** Keep your Google account secure, keep your treehouse's parent code (the PIN that keeps kids from leaving the phone screen) to yourself, and don't share sign-in QR codes. Sign out any device you lose, give away, or no longer trust. If you think someone has gotten into your treehouse, tell us right away.
4. You're responsible for everything that happens in your treehouse, including what your kids and any guests say and do on Treeline phones.
5. You and your household must follow our [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use.md).

2. Some families may use phone adapters or hand-built Treeline phones. You use any such hardware at your own risk. We can't guarantee it's safe, reliable, or suitable for your home, especially hardware you build or modify yourself.

## Ending your account

1. You can leave Treeline at any time from the parent app's Settings page. A parent can choose either:
   * **Leave the Treeline:** your strings end, the families you were strung with are told, and your phones stop working. Your call records are kept until your call-history setting runs out, then erased.
   * **Delete everything:** the same, and your treehouse, phones, strings, and call records are erased at once.

   Either way, other treehouses can no longer call yours. You can also ask us by email to do it for you. Our [Privacy Policy](privacy.md#when-you-delete-data-or-leave) explains what happens to your data.
2. We can suspend or remove any treehouse, or turn off any string, at any time and for any reason. This is especially true if we think a treehouse is breaking these terms or our Acceptable Use Policy, or is putting a kid at risk. When it's practical, we'll tell you why.
