# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

Treeline is built for kids, so we keep its privacy rules simple: **parents are in charge, we collect as little as possible, and we never sell or share your family's data for advertising.** This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

When we say "**we**" or "**us**", we mean Jordan Miller, who builds and runs Treeline. Words like "treehouse" and "string" mean the same thing they mean in our [Terms of Service](terms.md).

## The short version

* **Treeline can't call 911** or any number outside Treeline. Kids need another way to reach help.
* Only parents have accounts. Kids never create an account or type in personal information.
* **Calls are not recorded.** We keep a call log: which treehouse called which, when, and for how long. It's kept for 90 days unless your family picks a different time, from 7 days to a year.
* No ads, no tracking across other sites, and no sale of data. Ever.
* You can see your household's information, change it, or ask us to delete it at any time.

## Kids' privacy

Treeline is meant for kids, including kids under 13, so we've designed it around the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the idea behind it: parents should know and control what happens to their kids' information.

* **Parents set everything up.** A parent signs in, sets up the treehouse, approves each string, and signs in each phone by scanning a QR code. Kids can't join Treeline, add a connection, or change settings on their own.
* **We don't ask kids for personal information.** Kids don't have usernames, profiles, or passwords on Treeline. They pick up a phone and dial a 4-digit treehouse number.
* **What a kid's use creates:** their voice travels live through Treeline during a call and isn't stored. Treeline logs that the call happened (see "Call history" below). Treeline doesn't know which kid in a household made or answered a call, because calls belong to the treehouse, not to individual kids.
* **Parents can review, delete, and stop.** You can see your treehouse's call history in the app, shorten how long it's kept, sign phones out, turn off strings, and delete your treehouse completely from the app. See [Your choices and rights](#your-choices-and-rights).
* We don't use anything about your kids for advertising, profiling, or anything other than running Treeline.

## What we collect and why

Our rule is to collect only what Treeline needs to work.

### Parent sign-in

You sign in with Google. Treeline asks Google only for your email address and the fact that Google has verified it. We don't receive your name, profile picture, Google password, Gmail, contacts, or anything else in your Google account. When you set up a treehouse, you can type in your name if you'd like other grown-ups in your household to see it. It's optional.

We use your email address to sign you in, show who's in your household, and contact you about your treehouse or about important changes to Treeline.

**Beta invite check:** After you sign in with Google for the first time, you have to enter a beta invite code. Until you pass that check, we don't save anything about you to Treeline's database. Your sign-in waits in the server's memory for up to 15 minutes and is then forgotten. If you pass the check but don't finish setting up a treehouse, your sign-in is deleted after a day. The only other exception is the short-lived server logs described below.

### Your treehouse

* The treehouse name you choose, its 4-digit number, and its time zone
* The grown-ups in your treehouse: each one's email address, optional name, and whether they're a parent or a sitter
* Your strings (which treehouses you're connected to, who asked, who approved each one, and whether it's paused)
* Your quiet hours, call limits, and other settings
* Your parent code, stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form we can't read back
* The phones signed in to your treehouse: the name you give each one, what kind of phone it is, a generated phone account with its password stored only in hashed form, whether it's online, and when it last checked in
* If you turn on notifications: the address your browser gives us for sending them
* When a grown-up agreed to these policies and our Terms, which version, and that they understood Treeline can't call 911
* Notes the app shows you, such as a new string request, and a log of what grown-ups in your treehouse changed (for example, approving a string)

Your phone book isn't stored separately. It's a printable list made from your strings when you open it.

We use this to connect calls only between treehouses with approved strings, apply your quiet hours and limits, and run your parent app.

### Call history

For each call attempt, we log which treehouse called which (or the number dialed, if it wasn't a strung treehouse), which of your phones made or answered it, when it started, how long it lasted, and how it ended (for example answered, busy, no answer, quiet hours, or a limit reached). This gives parents a full record of calls to and from their treehouse.

**We do not record, store, or transcribe what anyone says on a call.** Call audio passes through our servers only to connect the two treehouses, live.

### Server and security logs

Like any website, our servers keep technical logs, including IP addresses, the times pages and calls were requested, and errors. We use them to keep Treeline running, fix bugs, and spot abuse or break-in attempts. The web server's logs never include the secret parts of web addresses, such as sign-in tokens.

Most of these logs are kept only in the server's memory, never on disk. They're erased whenever the server restarts, and the oldest are dropped sooner once they reach a small size limit. The phone system's own log is kept on disk for up to about five weeks.

### Cookies

We use only the cookies Treeline needs to keep you signed in and secure. We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party tracking pixels. Every script, font, and image in the app comes from Treeline's own server, and the app's security settings block anything from anywhere else.

### Email to us

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you and remember the history if you write again.

## What other families can see

Treeline is a shared network, so some information is visible to the families you connect with:

* **Treehouses you have a string with, or that you ask for one,** can see your treehouse name and number. They can also see whether your side has approved the string and whether you've paused it. If you leave Treeline, they're told that your treehouse left.
* **Each treehouse's call history includes calls with other treehouses.** If your kid calls a friend, that call shows up in your history *and* in your friend's family's history: the treehouse name and number, the time, and how long it lasted.
* Other families never see why a call to you didn't connect (for example, your quiet hours or limits), or calls your kids tried to make to a treehouse you aren't strung with.
* Other families can't see your grown-ups' names or email addresses, your phones' names, your settings, your phone book, or calls you had with anyone else.

## Who else handles your data

We use a few service providers to run Treeline. They may handle your data only so they can provide their service to us.

| Provider | What they do for Treeline | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services (AWS) | Hosting, encrypted database backups, and storage of secret keys (Oregon, United States) | All Treeline data |
| Amazon Simple Email Service (part of AWS) | Sending alert emails to parents | Your email address and the alert's text |
| Google | Parent sign-in | Your email address and whether Google has verified it |
| Your browser's push service (for example Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, or Mozilla for Firefox) | Delivering notifications to your phone or computer, only if you turn them on | The notification, sent encrypted |
| GitHub | Hosting Treeline's source code and build process | No household data |

## When we look at or share your information

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We look at or share it only:

* **To run Treeline,** using the providers listed above.
* **To run the service day to day.** Treeline's admin page shows us each treehouse's name and number, when it was created, how many phones it has and when they last checked in, and its grown-ups' email addresses and roles. It never shows us calls, strings, quiet hours, phone names, or anything else in your family's records.
* **To fix problems.** Usually we can fix a problem without looking at anyone's data. If we need to look at your treehouse to help with something you asked about, we'll ask you first. If something breaks in a way that needs it, we'll look at only the minimum needed to fix it.
* **To keep kids and Treeline safe.** If we get a report that Treeline is being misused, or we see signs of abuse or a security problem, we may review the affected treehouse's account information and call history. If a child may be in danger, we may contact the parents involved or the authorities.
* **When the law requires it.** We share information with law enforcement only when compelled by valid legal process, such as a warrant, subpoena, or court order, or in a genuine emergency involving risk of serious harm. When the law allows, we'll tell you before we share.
* **If Treeline changes hands.** We have no plans to sell or transfer Treeline. If it ever happens, we'll tell you before your information moves or becomes subject to a different privacy policy, and you'll be able to delete your treehouse first.

## How long we keep things

* **Call history:** 90 days, unless your family chooses a different time in the app (anywhere from 7 days to a year). Older calls are deleted automatically.
* **Log of grown-ups' changes:** One year.
* **Treehouse and parent information:** Kept while your treehouse is active.
* **Server logs:** In memory until the server restarts, and the phone system's log for up to about five weeks (see "Server and security logs" above).
* **Backups:** We back up each treehouse's data separately and keep backups for 7 days. After that, they're deleted automatically.

## When you delete data or leave

* If you shorten your call history setting, calls older than the new setting are deleted from Treeline. Copies may remain in backups for up to about 7 more days.
* If you choose **Delete everything**, your treehouse, grown-ups, phones, strings, and call records are removed from Treeline right away, and its strings to other treehouses end. Copies in backups are deleted within 7 days.
* If you choose **Leave the Treeline** instead, your strings end and your phones stop working right away, but your call records are kept until your call-history setting runs out. Then they're deleted too.
* Calls involving your treehouse may still appear in the call history of the other treehouse on the call, until that family's own history setting deletes them. After you delete everything, those calls no longer show your treehouse's name.

## How we protect your data

* All connections to Treeline use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Data is encrypted at rest. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server, and only Treeline's maintainer holds the key to open them.

* Phone passwords, sign-in tokens, and the parent code are stored only in hashed form.
* Each treehouse's data is stored separately from every other treehouse's data.
* Secret keys and passwords for Treeline's systems are kept in a dedicated secrets store, not in the database or the public code.
* Our server can write backups but can't read, change, or delete them, which helps protect backups if the server is ever compromised.
* No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell affected parents promptly and explain what happened.

## Your choices and rights

Whatever state or country you live in, you can:

* **See** your treehouse's information and call history in the parent app, or ask us for a copy.
* **Correct** your information in the app, or ask us to correct it.
* **Delete** your call history (by shortening how long it's kept) or your whole treehouse (Settings, then **Delete everything**).
* **Stop collection** by signing phones out, turning off strings, or leaving Treeline. If you ask us to stop collecting information about your household, your treehouse will be deleted, because Treeline can't connect calls without it.
* **Ask questions** or **complain** to us, or to your state attorney general or the Federal Trade Commission.

To make a request, email [jdmiller2010@gmail.com](mailto:jdmiller2010@gmail.com) from the Google account you use with Treeline, so we can confirm it's you. We'll respond within 30 days.

Treeline is run in the United States, for families in the United States. Information is stored in the United States (Oregon).
