# Acceptable Use Policy

*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

Treeline works because the families on it trust each other. These rules apply to every parent, every kid, and anyone else who uses a Treeline phone. Parents are responsible for making sure their household follows them.

## Not okay on Treeline

Don't use Treeline to:

* **Bully, harass, threaten, or scare anyone.** This includes prank calls meant to upset someone and calling over and over after someone has asked you to stop.
* **Contact a kid behind their parents' backs.** Strings exist so both families agree before any calls happen. Don't try to get around that. For example, don't sign a phone in to another family's treehouse, and don't approve a string for a family you don't actually know.
* **Get around quiet hours, strings, or other parent controls,** or help a kid do so.
* **Share sign-in QR codes or invite codes** with anyone outside your household, unless we've asked you to invite someone.
* **Record a call** without telling everyone on it.
* **Connect Treeline to the regular phone network** or to other calling systems, or use it to reach anyone outside Treeline.
* **Make automated or mass calls,** or use bots or scripts with Treeline.
* **Sell, advertise, or make money** with the Treeline service.
* **Collect information about other households** beyond what the app shows you.
* **Break into or interfere with Treeline.** This includes trying to get into another treehouse, probing or attacking our servers, overloading the service, or spreading malware. If you find a security hole, please report it privately (see below) instead of using it.
* **Pretend to be someone else** or lie to us or to other families.
* **Break the law.**

If a treehouse breaks these rules, we may turn off its strings, sign out its phones, or remove it from Treeline. We may do this without warning when a kid's safety is involved.

## Reporting a problem

If something on Treeline worries you, email [jdmiller2010@gmail.com](mailto:jdmiller2010@gmail.com). Examples include a call that upset your kid, a treehouse you don't recognize, or something that looks like a security problem. Include the treehouse numbers and the date and time if you have them. We won't tell the other household who reported them.

**If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 from a regular phone. Treeline can't call 911 or reach emergency services.**
