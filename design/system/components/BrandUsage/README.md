# BrandUsage

A showcase of how the logo and illustrations are placed: approved and forbidden backgrounds, clear space, which icon to use at which size, the lockup, and an illustration with its text spacing. The full rules are in the brand book under **Logo and app icon** and **Using illustrations**.

- Not a component to import.
- The logo art here is drawn in Daylight colors in both themes, because the files are fixed colors. In the app, use `Brand` (theme-aware) and `Illustration` (drawn from tokens).
