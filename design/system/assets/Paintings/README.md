# Paintings

Five small watercolor paintings for the artist's layer (design system README, "The artist's layer", "Inside a card, and paintings"): seedling, mushroom, fern, meadow, dandelion. Pencil line, one to three washes, paper showing through; light-theme colors baked in. In the product they are drawn from `ui/js/doodles.js` with a dark variant. They stand on a card's top edge only where the room already exists, and never add space for themselves. The fern and dandelion are drawn hanging and are shown flipped to stand.
