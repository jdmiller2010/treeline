# Paintings

Five small watercolor paintings in the artist's hand: seedling, mushroom, fern, meadow, dandelion. Pencil line, one to three washes, paper showing through; light-theme colors baked in. The fern and dandelion are drawn hanging and are shown flipped to stand. They used to stand on a card's top edge in the artist's layer; at about 60px tall they don't fit the sections gap the layer now uses (design system README, "The artist's layer"), so they are kept here for plates and future use and are no longer drawn by `ui/js/doodles.js`.
