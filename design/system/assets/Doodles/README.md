# Doodles

Eighteen tiny pencil-and-wash drawings for the artist's layer (see the design system README, "The artist's layer"). Each is an SVG with the washes and pencil line baked in using the light theme's colors, so they open as they are in Inkscape or Illustrator. In the product they are drawn live, with theme colors, from `ui/js/doodles.js`.

`snail` · `ladybug` · `tuft` · `sprout` · `acorn` · `pebbles` · `mushroom` · `pinecone` · `owl` · `bluebird` · `tulip` · `fern` · `bow` (stand on a surface) · `butterfly` · `bee` · `seeds` · `maple` (fly) · `blossom` (clings).

The line is `rgb(94,85,70)`, 1.3 wide, round caps, drawn on a 4x grid in these files. Washes sit slightly offset (1.2 across, 1 down) from the line, like a real wash that did not quite hit the pencil. Never place one over text or a control, and never use more than two or three on a screen.
