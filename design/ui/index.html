Actions

Button

A labeled action. Secondary is the default; Primary (`canopy`) appears at most once per view, for the thing the screen is for. components/Button.html · rules

IconButton

A round button holding a single icon, for compact repeated actions in rows and toolbars. components/IconButton.html · rules

Status

Tag

A small pill of translucent wash for a state, a category or a count. components/Tag.html · rules

Presence

A dot and a word showing what a kid's phone is doing right now. components/Presence.html · rules

Avatar

A friend's initials on a watercolor pebble, with an optional presence dot. components/Avatar.html · rules

Badge

A small count or dot that says "something here needs you." components/Badge.html · rules

Steps

Where you are in a short setup flow, and how much is left. components/Steps.html · rules

ProgressBar

How far along something is that takes a few seconds or more: a phone update, a search for the phone. components/ProgressBar.html · rules

Skeleton

Soft placeholders that hold the shape of a list or card while it loads. components/Skeleton.html · rules

Icons

A 24-grid line icon drawn with `currentColor`, so it takes the color of the text around it. components/Icon.html · rules

Inputs

Switch

An instant on/off setting with a label and an optional description. components/Switch.html · rules

Checkbox

A choice that's saved with a form, alone or in a list. components/Checkbox.html · rules

RadioGroup

Pick exactly one of a few choices, each of which needs a sentence to explain it. components/RadioGroup.html · rules

TextField

A labeled single-line input with an optional hint or error. components/TextField.html · rules

Textarea

Several lines of text: a note with a request, a report, feedback. components/Textarea.html · rules

PasswordField

A password with a show/hide button. components/PasswordField.html · rules

CodeInput

One box per digit, for pairing a phone and for sign-in codes. components/CodeInput.html · rules

Select

Pick one option from a short list. components/Select.html · rules

TimeField

Enter a time of day. components/TimeField.html · rules

TimeRange

A start and end time, like quiet hours or homework time. components/TimeRange.html · rules

SegmentedControl

Switch between two to four views or modes of the same thing. components/SegmentedControl.html · rules

Slider

Pick a value on a range where the exact number matters less than the feel: ringer volume, call length. components/Slider.html · rules

Containers

Card

A sheet of paper laid on the papyrus that groups related content. components/Card.html · rules

DescriptionList

Label and value pairs on a detail page: a phone's model, last check-in, Wi-Fi. components/DescriptionList.html · rules

Disclosure

A heading that opens to show more: help answers, advanced settings. components/Disclosure.html · rules

StatTile

One number that matters, big, with what it means. components/StatTile.html · rules

Feedback

Alert

An inline notice on the page: something the parent should know or fix. components/Alert.html · rules

Toast

A short, passing confirmation after the parent does something. components/Toast.html · rules

Patterns

FriendRow

One approved friend in a list: avatar, name, household, speed dial and what their phone is doing. components/FriendRow.html · rules

ApprovalCard

Shows the two halves of a mutual approval: a call connects only when both households say yes. components/ApprovalCard.html · rules

QuietHours

A schedule when a kid's phone rests: times, days and an on/off switch. components/QuietHours.html · rules

CallLogItem

One line in a parent's call log: who called whom, when, and how it ended. components/CallLogItem.html · rules

Nature

Illustration

Treeline's spot illustrations: ink line art over loose watercolor washes, drawn from tokens so they work in both themes. components/Illustration.html · rules

EmptyState

A calm, centered moment for screens with nothing in them yet: an illustration, a title, one sentence, and at most one action. components/EmptyState.html · rules

LeafDivider

A hairline that fades in from both sides and meets at a small sprig of two leaves. components/LeafDivider.html · rules

LeafLoader

Three leaves blowing across two gusts of wind: Treeline's loading and connecting indicator. components/LeafLoader.html · rules

App layout

AppShell

The frame every parent screen lives in: a top bar, navigation and the main area, rearranged for phones, tablets and desktop. components/AppShell.html · rules

Live: app.html is a working screen. Resize your window to watch tabs become a rail and then a sidebar. Try Friends (list and detail) and Add friend (a sheet).

AppBar

The top of every screen: where you are and the one or two things you can do here. components/AppBar.html · rules

NavBar

Main navigation between the parent app's top-level places. One component, three looks. components/NavBar.html · rules

Tabs

Switch between sections of one page, like a friend's Overview, Calls and Settings. components/Tabs.html · rules

Menu

A short list of actions behind a button: the account menu, or "more" on a row. components/Menu.html · rules

Sheet

A focused question or short form over the current screen: approve a friend, edit quiet hours, confirm a pause. components/Sheet.html · rules

ListDetail

The layout for Friends and Calls: a list, and the details of whatever row you picked. components/ListDetail.html · rules

PageTemplates

Every parent screen is one of four templates. Pick the template first, then fill it with components. components/PageTemplates.html · rules

Layout

A full screen

Built only from layout classes: .tl-page, .tl-stack, .tl-split, .tl-grid, .tl-actions. Rules in LAYOUT.md; markup in components/Layout.html.
