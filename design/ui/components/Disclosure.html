What if Sam’s phone loses Wi-Fi?

Calls can’t reach it until it reconnects. You’ll see “offline” on Home, and the phone tries again every minute.

Who can my kid call?

Only friends both families approved, plus family you add. Nobody else can ring the phone.

Can I listen to calls?

No. Treeline never records calls. You see who called whom, when and for how long.

How do quiet hours work?

During quiet hours calls wait. The phone won’t ring, and the caller hears a soft “try later” tone.
