# Treeline design system: notes for Claude Code

This folder is the Treeline design system. Build every parent-app screen from it; don't invent new styles.

- `ui/` is the plain-HTML kit: `css/tokens.css` + `css/treeline.css` (self-hosted fonts in `fonts/`), `js/treeline.js` (menus, sheets, toasts, tabs, code boxes, sliders, password toggle), `components/*.html` (exact markup for every variant) and `components/*.md` (the rules for each). `ui/app.html` is a working screen that uses the shell and all four page templates. Open `ui/index.html` to see everything.
- `system/` is the source: `README.md` (brand book: color, type, watercolor, logo and illustration rules), `Layout.md` (spacing, sizing, app layout, phone vs desktop for every component), `tokens.json`, `components/bundle.js` (React versions on `window.Treeline`, typed in `components/index.d.ts`) and `assets/` (logos, exports, illustrations, icons).

## Rules to follow when writing UI

1. Copy markup from `ui/components/<Name>.html` and keep its classes and nesting. Use tokens (`var(--space-4)`, `var(--ink)`), never raw values.
2. Every screen sits in `.tl-app` (the shell: `.tl-app > .tl-app__layout > header.tl-appbar, main.tl-app__main, nav.tl-nav`) or has `class="tl-root"` on `<body>`. Components use container queries, so without one the phone layout never applies.
3. Sheets and the `.tl-toasts` region are the last children of `.tl-app`, never inside main. No `backdrop-filter`, `transform` or `filter` on the app bar.
4. Navigation is always Home, Friends, Calls, Quiet hours. One `h1` per screen, in the app bar. Each screen is one template: Overview, List and detail, Settings form or Focused task (a sheet).
5. No light-green backgrounds; green (`canopy`) is for accents and the primary button only. One toned card per screen. Every phone icon is the rotary phone.
6. Test at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 in both themes (`data-theme="light"` / `"dark"` on `<html>`): nothing may scroll sideways, and the app bar title lines up with the first card.
7. **No 911, said plainly.** The softphone says it with the `RotaryPhone`'s own notice under the phone (a small pill with a berry warning triangle: "Can’t call 911 or outside numbers"), always there and never turned off or replaced with an `Alert`; the How to dial guide has a step for it too. The parent app says it during setup (the parent ticks "I understand"), on Home, and in About. Never hide it in settings.
8. **Footer in every app** (`footer()` / `footerLinks()` in `web/shared/ui.js`, `.tl-footer`): a small line at the bottom in `ink-muted`: "Made by Jordan Miller · © 2026", "Support the project: ☕ Buy me a coffee" (the `coffee` icon; links to `https://buymeacoffee.com/jdmiller`), "✉ Email Jordan" (the `mail` icon; `mailto:jdmiller2010@gmail.com`) and "Open-source licenses" (a plain page built from `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, with Treeline's own licenses first).
9. **The softphone screen is an ordinary Treeline page**, not a room: the brand mark and name with the tagline under it (not a link), a quiet-time `Tag` when there are quiet hours, the treehouse's name as the one `h1`, a `Presence` for what the phone is doing with one muted line under it, the berry `RotaryPhone` (with its own no-911 notice) on a soft `wash-sun` glow with the `CardFile` of approved strings beside it on the same baseline (directly under it and centred below 600px), one secondary "How to dial" button that opens a `Sheet` of the guide's nine pictures (`design/ui/illustrations/guide/`) with compact `Steps`, Back and Next, and the footer. Words left and the phone right on landscape screens of 900px and up. Pass the treehouse's `number`; fill the card file with the strings. Listen for `tl-rotary-digit` (four digits make a call), `tl-rotary-hook` (lift answers or starts dialing, hang-up ends the call) and `tl-rotary-pulse` (play a click). Don't add a number pad, caller ID or a display of digits. (`SoftphoneDesk`, the room, stays in the design system but the app doesn't use it.)
